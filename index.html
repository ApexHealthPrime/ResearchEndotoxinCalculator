What Do These Numbers Mean?
EU
EU means Endotoxin Units. This is how laboratories report bacterial endotoxin.
EU/vial
The total amount of measured endotoxin associated with the entire vial.
EU/mg
How much endotoxin corresponds to each milligram of peptide.
EU/dose
How much endotoxin is calculated to be in the amount you are actually taking.
EU/kg
Your endotoxin exposure adjusted for your body weight. The FDA/USP reference is 5 EU/kg.
FDA/USP 5 EU/kg Reference
The general endotoxin reference used by this calculator for ordinary non-intrathecal parenteral exposure. The FDA has not evaluated the specific peptide, vial, or batch.
Why does dose matter?
You usually take only part of a vial. The endotoxin in your actual dose can therefore be much lower than the total EU reported for the entire vial.
Why does weight matter?
The reference is based on body weight, so the calculator automatically converts your weight and does the math for you.
Frequently Asked Questions
What is bacterial endotoxin?
Bacterial endotoxins are substances released from certain bacteria. In injectable products, endotoxin levels are a key quality control measurement. Laboratory test results appear on a Certificate of Analysis (COA).
What does "WITHIN FDA/USP ENDOTOXIN REFERENCE" mean?
It means the calculated endotoxin exposure from your dose is within the FDA/USP 5 EU/kg reference used by this calculator. It does not mean that the FDA has evaluated or approved the specific product — it refers only to the endotoxin calculation, not to sterility, peptide purity, or any other aspect.
What if I take it every day?
The 5 EU/kg reference is based on the amount administered within one hour. A once-daily injection should not be multiplied by 7, 30, or 365 and compared with the 5 EU/kg one-hour reference. Monday's dose and Tuesday's dose are separate administration periods. However, multiple doses within the same hour should be added together.
What is a COA?
COA stands for Certificate of Analysis — a laboratory document reporting test results for a peptide batch. Look for the "Endotoxin" line to find the EU value to enter in this calculator.
Is this for intrathecal use?
No. This calculator uses the 5 EU/kg reference for ordinary non-intrathecal parenteral administration. Do not use this calculator for intrathecal administration, which has a much stricter reference limit.
